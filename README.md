# Pacific Sweep

https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/Holqang/pacific-sweep/blob/main/now.html

The subtitle says **Sky first, then the airport**.

Clear the planes over Hong Kong. A gold P sometimes drops and raises your guns. A blue crate is supply for the army. When the sky is empty, you fight on the ground for the airport. Left and right move, Up jumps, Space shoots, Shift throws a grenade.
